# Warriors Orochi

## What does randomization do to this game?
The officers who normally join you after a story stage or Gaiden objective are shuffled into the
multiworld. Each story still starts with its three default characters; everyone else only joins
when an Archipelago item for them arrives.

Received characters can be used in Free Mode, and in Story Mode in the story that normally recruits them.

## What is a check?
- Clearing each of the 8 stages of the Shu, Wei, Wu and Samurai stories (32 checks).
- Clearing Gaiden 2-7 of each story (24 checks, on by default).
- Optional: the four Last Gaiden (Gaiden 8) stages.
- Optional: completing the Gaiden objectives that normally unlock a character.
- Optional: getting 100 / 200 / 300 / 500 / 750 / 1000 KOs in a single battle (KO Milestone Checks).
- Optional: getting each character's 4th weapon (4th Weapon Checks). In vanilla these drop on Hard
  in 3-star or harder stages; the check is sent when you return to camp.

## What is the goal?
Clear a number of stories (Stage 8), set by the Stories Required option (1-4, default 4).

## What are the items?
Characters, plus "1000 Growth Points" filler when there are more checks than characters.
With Trap Chance above 0, some filler becomes traps instead:
- Musou Drain Trap: empties your Musou gauge.
- Low Health Trap: drops you to 10% health.

Traps wait until you are in a battle.

## Locked Stages
On by default (Lock Out-of-Logic Stages): the client hides every story and Gaiden stage you are not
in logic for yet, so you can't play ahead of your items. A stage needs enough of its story's
characters (Story Character Requirement), plus its stage items when Stage Locks is on. Hidden stages
reappear as soon as you have what they need; stages you already cleared are never hidden.
Keep the client connected while you play, since it is what hides the stages.

## Stage Locks
With Stage Locks on, each story has 7 "Progressive <Story> Stage" items. Stage N and Gaiden N of a
story only open once you have N-1 of that story's items and have cleared the stage before it.
Stage 1 of every story is always open. Works in both Story Mode and Free Mode.

## Ability Shuffle
With Ability Shuffle on, the 18 army-wide abilities (Vitality, Focus, Potence, Fortitude, Impulse,
Cavalier, Karma, Power, Speed, Technique, Boost, Awakening, Acclaim, Recuperate, Conserve, Adrenalin,
Recover, Refill) are shuffled. Earning one in-game sends "Earn Ability: <name>"; the ability stays hidden
until its "Ability: <name>" item arrives, and any levels earned meanwhile are kept.
The Earn Ability checks only hold filler, since exactly how the game hands abilities out is not mapped yet.

## In-Game Notices
On by default. While you play a stage, the client rewrites the game's own end-of-stage notice
("<officer> has become available for use.") to say what that stage's check holds, for example
"Sent Hookshot to Alex!" or "Found Okuni!". Stages where nobody joins use the
"A new scenario has become available." notice instead.

## Death Link
With Death Link on, falling in battle kills the other linked players, and their deaths drop you
to 1 health (held there) so the next hit finishes you. Deaths received outside a battle are ignored.
